Rules at a glance
  1. Reach the edge opposite your start to win. On your turn: move one square (orthogonally), or spend a wall.
  2. A wall spans two squares and sits in a groove between cells. It must not overlap or cross another wall.
  3. No wall may seal any player off completely — everyone must keep a path to their goal.
  4. When adjacent to an opponent you may jump straight over it; if that square is blocked by a wall, edge, or pawn, you may jump diagonally to its side instead.
  5. 2-player: 10 walls each · 4-player: 5 walls each. First to reach the opposite edge wins.
  6. Walls are painted in the color of the player who placed them — read the map of blockades at a glance.
  7. Walls: hover along a row groove for a horizontal wall, along a column groove for a vertical one.
